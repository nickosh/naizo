6. Brand Logo Concept, Cultural Symbolism & Status Pill Redesign Philosophy 丸に三つ石 (Maru ni Mitsuishi) · 品 (Hin / Shina) · 内蔵 / 内臓 (Naizō)

1. Architectural Metaphor (丸 + 三つ石)
Outer Ring (丸): The single .html file as a sealed, dependency-free boundary. Three Stones (三つ石): The built-in engine (State + Logic + View & HTML + CSS + JS) inside the file.
2. Kanji 「品」 & Kamon 「丸に三つ石」
Forms 品 — 品質 (Hinshitsu, quality), 作品 (Sakuhin, finished artifact), and 品格 (Hinkaku, zero-bloat dignity) — and the Japanese heraldic crest 丸に三つ石 (three interlocking foundation stones in a circle of harmony 和).
3. Gestalt & Collision Guardrails
Three upright rounded squares (rx="4.5" = ~20.5% of 22u width) avoid 3-circle collisions (Asana, Banner of Peace, XLR pinouts, GSI 史跡) and 4-diamond collisions (Figma Component, VS Code Extensions) while staying crisp at 16px–20px.
4. Frosted Glass & Cycle-Boundary Motion
True translucent frosted glass (blur(12px) saturate(180%)) with a whisper-ring shadow (border: transparent), 4-corner frosted drop placeholders remembered across sessions via naizodb_ui_<suffix> in localStorage, and cycle-boundary animation handoff (requestDotAnim() — every logo animation plays at least 1 full cycle and always finishes its current cycle before switching states or returning to static).
